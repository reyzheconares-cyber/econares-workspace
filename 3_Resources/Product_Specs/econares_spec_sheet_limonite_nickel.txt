================================================================================
MATERIAL CHARACTERISTICS
================================================================================

Ore Type:               Limonite (iron-rich nickel ore)
Color/Appearance:        Yellowish-brown to reddish-brown laterite
Texture:                Friable, earthy laterite
Formation:              Weathered nickel silicate ore (saprolite overprint)
Suitability:            Ideal for NPI (Nickel Pig Iron) production via blast furnace
                        or rotary kiln-electric furnace (RKEF) route
Blending Recommendation: Suited for blending with higher-grade Indonesian ore
                        (1.5–2.0% Ni) to optimize NPI feed chemistry
