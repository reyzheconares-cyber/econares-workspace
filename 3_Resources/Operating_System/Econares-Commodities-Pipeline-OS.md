SECTION 2: PIPELINE STAGES
================================================================================

THE PIPELINE CONSISTS OF SEVEN STAGES. EACH STAGE HAS ENTRY CRITERIA, ACTIONS,
EXIT CRITERIA, AND REQUIRED TOOLS. ALL DEAL PROGRESS IS LOGGED IN HUBSPOT.

STAGE 0 — PROSPECT RESEARCH
ENTRY: MARKET INTELLIGENCE IDENTIFIES A BUYER OR SUPPLIER
ACTIONS:
  1. IDENTIFY DECISION-MAKER (C-LEVEL OR HEAD OF PROCUREMENT ONLY)
  2. GATHER COMPANY INTELLIGENCE: REGISTRY FILINGS, ANNUAL REPORTS, TRADE DATA
  3. MAP BUYING PARAMETERS: VOLUME, GRADE SPECS, PRICE BENCHMARK, LOGISTICS
  4. CHECK EXISTING HUBSPOT RECORD — DO NOT DUPLICATE CONTACTS
  5. CREATE HUBSPOT CONTACT RECORD WITH MINIMAL PROPERTIES:
     EMAIL, FIRST NAME, LAST NAME, JOB TITLE, COMPANY, PHONE, CITY, COUNTRY,
     LIFECYCLE STAGE: LEAD
  6. CREATE HUBSPOT DEAL RECORD AT $0 VALUE WITH STAGE: APPOINTMENT SCHEDULED
EXIT: CONTACT CREATED IN HUBSPOT WITH DECISION-MAKER IDENTIFIED
TOOLS: LINKEDIN, Panjiva, WITS, HUBSPOT, GOOGLE SHEETS

STAGE 1 — FIRST OUTREACH
ENTRY: HUBSPOT CONTACT READY
ACTIONS:
  1. CHOOSE CHANNEL: CALL / SMS / EMAIL / WHATSAPP
  2. DRAFT PERSONALIZED MESSAGE — NO MGB/DENR MENTION YET
  3. LOG ACTIVITY IN HUBSPOT (CALL LOG OR EMAIL ENGAGEMENT NOTE)
  4. FOLLOW-UP SEQUENCE: CALL 1 + EMAIL 1 WITHIN 5 BUSINESS DAYS
  5. IF NO RESPONSE AFTER 2 ATTEMPTS, MOVE TO LIGHT TOUCH NURTURE SEQUENCE
EXIT: BUYER RESPONDS WITH INTEREST OR DECLINES
TOOLS: HUBSPOT, GOOGLE WORKSPACE (GMAIL), WHATSAPP / SMS

STAGE 2 — QUALIFICATION
ENTRY: BUYER HAS RESPONDED AND SHOWN INTEREST
ACTIONS:
  1. CONFIRM VOLUME REQUIREMENTS (WMT/MONTH OR TOTAL CONTRACT VOLUME)
  2. CONFIRM GRADE SPECIFICATIONS (NI%, FE%, CU%, ASH%, SULFUR%, ETC.)
  3. CONFIRM PRICE BENCHMARK REFERENCE (LME, COPPER SX-EW, BRENT CRUDE)
  4. CONFIRM LOGISTICS PREFERENCE: CFR, CIF, FOB, DAP
  5. CONFIRM PAYMENT TERMS: LC, TT, DOCOL, OPEN ACCOUNT
  6. CONFIRM BUYING ENTITY: HEAD OFFICE, SUBSIDIARY, OR TRADING HOUSE
  7. FLAG MGB/DENR COMPLIANCE REQUIREMENT — DISCUSS NOW (NOT BEFORE)
  8. UPDATE HUBSPOT DEAL: VALUE, STAGE: QUALIFIED TO BUY
  9. ADD QUALIFICATION NOTES TO HUBSPOT CONTACT RECORD
EXIT: SPEC SHEET CONFIRMED AND DEAL ENTERED IN HUBSPOT WITH MONETARY VALUE
TOOLS: HUBSPOT, SPEC SHEET TEMPLATE, COMMODITY PRICE BENCHMARKS

STAGE 3 — PROPOSAL AND PRICING
ENTRY: QUALIFICATION COMPLETE, SPECS CONFIRMED
ACTIONS:
  1. DRAFT SPECIFICATION SHEET (GRADE, VOLUME, ORIGIN, DELIVERY TERMS)
  2. RUN PRICE BENCHMARK: LME/BRENT/SPOT INDEX + PREMIUM/DISCOUNT MODEL
  3. CALCULATE DELIVERY COST: FOB + FREIGHT + INSURANCE + PORT CHARGES
  4. BUILD PRICING PROPOSAL: UNIT PRICE PER WMT, TOTAL DEAL VALUE
  5. INCLUDE PAYMENT TERM OFFER (LC AT SIGHT PREFERRED)
  6. ADD MGB/DENR COMPLIANCE CLAUSE — REQUIRED FOR ALL PH MINERAL EXPORTS
  7. SEND PROPOSAL TO BUYER — LOG SEND DATE IN HUBSPOT
  8. UPDATE HUBSPOT DEAL STAGE: PROPOSAL SENT
EXIT: PROPOSAL DELIVERED AND BUYER REVIEWING
TOOLS: HUBSPOT, GOOGLE DOCS, PRICE BENCHMARK DATA (LME, BRENT, INDEX)

STAGE 4 — NEGOTIATION
ENTRY: BUYER HAS REVIEWED PROPOSAL AND IS NEGOTIATING
ACTIONS:
  1. TRACK BUYER COUNTER-OFFERS AND OBJECTIONS
  2. REVISIT PRICING: HOLD MARGIN FLOOR, NEGOTIATE VOLUME DISCOUNT
  3. CLARIFY LOGISTICS: VESSEL TYPE, LOADING PORT, DELIVERY TIMELINE
  4. ALIGN ON PAYMENT TERM: ACCEPTABLE RANGE IS LC AT SIGHT TO 30 DAYS DOCOL
  5. INVOLVE SUPPLIER SIDE FOR VOLUME/FEED CONFIRMATION
  6. FLAG HIGH-RISK ACCOUNTS (SEE SECTION 4) — ESCALATE TO MANAGEMENT
  7. UPDATE HUBSPOT DEAL STAGE: NEGOTIATION IN PROGRESS
  8. LOG EVERY CALL, EMAIL, AND COUNTER-OFFER IN HUBSPOT ACTIVITY FEED
EXIT: COMMERCIAL TERMS AGREED — LOI / MOA STAGE
TOOLS: HUBSPOT, LOI TEMPLATE, PRICE BENCHMARK

STAGE 5 — CONTRACT AND LOI
ENTRY: COMMERCIAL TERMS AGREED
ACTIONS:
  1. ISSUE LETTER OF INTENT (LOI) OR MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT (MOA)
  2. LOI / MOA MUST INCLUDE:
     - COMMODITY, VOLUME, GRADE SPECS, PRICE, DELIVERY TERMS
     - PAYMENT TERM, DELIVERY SCHEDULE, INSPECTION CLAUSE
     - MGB/DENR COMPLIANCE AND EXPORT PERMIT CLAUSE
     - FORCE MAJEURE, DISPUTE RESOLUTION, GOVERNING LAW
  3. BUYER SIGNS LOI — SCAN AND UPLOAD TO HUBSPOT CONTACT RECORD
  4. UPDATE HUBSPOT DEAL STAGE: CONTRACT SENT
  5. SEND COPY TO SUPPLIER / MINE OPERATOR FOR COUNTER-SIGNATURE
EXIT: LOI / MOA FULLY EXECUTED BY ALL PARTIES
TOOLS: HUBSPOT, LOI/MOA TEMPLATE (GOOGLE DOCS)

STAGE 6 — EXECUTION AND LOGISTICS
ENTRY: LOI EXECUTED
ACTIONS:
  1. CONFIRM SUPPLIER READINESS: MINING PRODUCT TONNAGE, GRADE CERT
  2. ARRANGE EXPORT PERMIT: MGB MINING PERMIT + DENR ECC/ORE EXPORT CLEARANCE
  3. BOOK VESSEL / CHARTER: FOCUS ON PH TO CHINA OR PH TO JAPAN ROUTES
  4. COORDINATE WITH LOGISTICS PARTNER: TRUCKING, PORT HANDLING, SHIPMENT
  5. PROVIDE LOADING INSPECTION REPORT TO BUYER (SGS OR EQUIVALENT)
  6. TRACK SHIPMENT ETA — UPDATE BUYER REGULARLY
  7. SUBMIT SHIPPING DOCUMENTS TO BUYER'S BANK FOR PAYMENT RELEASE
  8. UPDATE HUBSPOT DEAL STAGE: CLOSED WON (OR CLOSED LOST IF FAILED)
EXIT: PAYMENT RECEIVED AND SHIPMENT DELIVERED
TOOLS: HUBSPOT, GOOGLE SHEETS (SHIPMENT TRACKER), SHIPPING DOCUMENT TEMPLATES

STAGE 7 — POST-DEAL AND RETENTION
ENTRY: DEAL CLOSED AND PAYMENT RECEIVED
ACTIONS:
  1. LOG FINAL PAYMENT CONFIRMATION IN HUBSPOT
  2. SEND POST-DEAL REPORT TO BUYER: TONNAGE DELIVERED, GRADE, VESSEL ETA
  3. REQUEST REFERRAL OR INTRO TO OTHER BUYING ENTITIES WITHIN THEIR NETWORK
  4. UPDATE HUBSPOT DEAL: STAGE = CLOSED WON, FINAL AMOUNT, CLOSE DATE
  5. SCHEDULE 30-DAY AND 90-DAY FOLLOW-UP CALL WITH BUYER
  6. ADD BUYER TO RE-ENGAGEMENT SEQUENCE FOR NEXT CONTRACT CYCLE
EXIT: BUYER RETAINED AND RE-ENGAGEMENT SCHEDULED
TOOLS: HUBSPOT, GOOGLE SHEETS, FOLLOW-UP CALL SCHEDULE

SECTION 4: RISK FRAMEWORK
================================================================================

HIGH RISK ACCOUNTS — REQUIRES ESCALATION AND DUE DILIGENCE BEFORE PROCEEDING:
- M&H MINING — FLAGGED HIGH RISK. PENDING BULK ORE LTD HK DUE DILIGENCE.
  DO NOT ADVANCE BEYOND STAGE 3 WITHOUT MANAGEMENT APPROVAL.

GENERAL RISK INDICATORS:
- REQUEST TO DEVIATE FROM STANDARD PAYMENT TERMS (LC AT SIGHT)
- UNVERIFIED OR SHELL COMPANY BUYER STRUCTURE
- UNABLE TO PROVIDE END-BUYER DOCUMENTATION
- DEMAND FOR PAYMENT BEFORE SHIPMENT WITHOUT LOI
- VAGUE OR CHANGING VOLUME / GRADE SPECIFICATIONS

MGB/DENR COMPLIANCE — NON-NEGOTIABLE:
- ALL PHILIPPINE MINERAL EXPORTS REQUIRE MGB MINING PERMIT AND DENR CLEARANCE
- EXPORT PERMIT MUST BE SECURED BEFORE VESSEL ARRIVAL AT LOADING PORT
- COMPLIANCE CLAUSE MUST APPEAR IN ALL LOI / MOA DOCUMENTS
- NEVER OMIT MGB/DENR DETAILS FROM CONTRACT DOCUMENTS
- ALWAYS FLAG COMPLIANCE ISSUES TO BUYER AT STAGE 2 (QUALIFICATION) OR EARLIER

SECTION 5: HUBSPOT CRM OPERATING STANDARDS
================================================================================

ALL DEALS AND CONTACTS MUST BE LOGGED IN HUBSPOT. THIS IS THE SYSTEM OF RECORD.

CONTACT CREATION RULES:
- ALWAYS USE MINIMAL PROPERTIES: EMAIL, FIRST NAME, LAST NAME, JOB TITLE,
  COMPANY, PHONE, CITY, COUNTRY, LIFECYCLE STAGE
- DO NOT DUPLICATE EXISTING CONTACTS — SEARCH FIRST BEFORE CREATING
- JOB TITLE MUST BE PROCUREMENT / C-LEVEL — NO INFO@ OR SALES@ CONTACTS

DEAL CREATION RULES:
- CREATE DEAL AT STAGE 0 WITH $0 VALUE
- UPDATE VALUE AS QUALIFICATION PROGRESSES
- CLOSE DATE = ESTIMATED DELIVERY DATE
- STAGE LABELS: APPOINTMENT SCHEDULED, QUALIFIED TO BUY, PROPOSAL SENT,
  NEGOTIATION IN PROGRESS, CONTRACT SENT, CLOSED WON, CLOSED LOST

ACTIVITY LOGGING:
- LOG EVERY CALL, EMAIL, SMS, AND MEETING IN HUBSPOT
- USE HUBSPOT NOTES FOR CALL SUMMARIES AND DECISION-MAKER DETAILS
- LOG PRICE PROPOSAL SENT DATE AND RESPONSE DATE

PIPELINE REVIEW SCHEDULE:
- WEEKLY: REVIEW ALL ACTIVE DEALS IN HUBSPOT PIPELINE
- WEEKLY: FOLLOW UP ON STALE DEALS (NO ACTIVITY IN 14+ DAYS)
- MONTHLY: PIPELINE VALUE REPORT — TOTAL ACTIVE, CLOSED WON, CLOSED LOST
