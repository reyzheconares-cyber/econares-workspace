================================================================================
        NOTES & ASSUMPTIONS
================================================================================

1. All prices are indicative and subject to final assay at loading.
2. "Iron Credit" of USD 6.00/WMT reflects value of 40% Fe content as blast 
   furnace ferrous feed in NPI operations. Actual iron credit negotiable.
3. TC/RC of USD 8–16/WMT reflects industry-standard NPI processing charges;
   exact rate to be agreed with buyer during contract negotiation.
4. Ocean freight rates are spot estimates (April 2026); contract rates differ.
5. FOB prices assume gross weight basis (no moisture adjustment).
   Net DMT price = WMT price × (1 - moisture%).
6. For the 0.5% Ni / 40% Fe Tawi-Tawi stockpile specifically:
   - INDICATIVE FOB Tawi-Tawi: USD 62–66/WMT
   - INDICATIVE CIF Rizhao/Lanshan: USD 69–73/WMT
   - This is the entry price for establishing off-take conversations.
