## 1. PURPOSE & SCOPE

### 1.1 Purpose
This Environmental Manual establishes ECONARES commitment to environmental stewardship in all commodity trading and logistics operations. It ensures compliance with Philippine environmental laws and regulations, including DENR, MGB, and EMB requirements.

### 1.2 Scope
Applies to all commodities and operations involving:
- Nickel/Copper Ore extraction and transport
- Diesel storage and handling
- Coal and PKS storage and loading
- Port and logistics operations
- Warehouse and depot activities

---

## 2. ENVIRONMENTAL POLICY

ECONARES is committed to:
- Full compliance with all applicable environmental laws and regulations
- Prevention of pollution from operations
- Minimization of environmental impacts from commodity handling
- Responsible use of natural resources
- Continuous improvement in environmental performance

Key Regulatory Frameworks:
- Republic Act 8749 (Clean Air Act)
- Republic Act 9275 (Clean Water Act)
- Republic Act 6969 (Toxic Substances and Hazardous Wastes Act)
- Presidential Decree 1586 (Environmental Impact Statement System)
- DENR MGB Regulations for Mining and Quarrying

Approved By: CEO, ECONARES | Review Frequency: Annually

## 4. COMMODITY-SPECIFIC ENVIRONMENTAL PROCEDURES

### 4.1 Nickel/Copper Ore
**Key Environmental Concerns:**
- Water pollution from sediment runoff
- Air quality (dust emissions)
- Land degradation from ore handling
- Tailings and waste rock management

**Required Environmental Compliance:**
- MGB-approved Mining Work Plan
- Environmental Protection and Enhancement Program (EPEP)
- Final Mine Rehabilitation and Decommissioning Plan (FMRDP)
- Annual EPEP implementation report to MGB
- Quarterly water quality monitoring
- Progressive rehabilitation of disturbed areas

**Water Management:**
- Sediment ponds and barriers at mine site perimeter
- Settling ponds for runoff before discharge
- Oil-water separators for equipment wash areas
- No direct discharge to natural water bodies without treatment

**Dust Control:**
- Water sprinkling on haul roads and handling areas
- Enclosed conveyors where feasible
- Tree planting on rehabilitated areas

**Ore Transport:**
- Covered trucks for ore transport
- Wheel wash stations at mine exit
- OTP must accompany each shipment
- Transport routes approved by LGU/MGB

### 4.2 Diesel Storage and Handling
**Key Environmental Concerns:**
- Fuel spills contaminating soil and water
- Vapor emissions
- Groundwater contamination from leaks

**Required Environmental Compliance:**
- DENR-EMB storage permit for bulk fuel facilities
- Spill Prevention, Control, and Countermeasure (SPCC) Plan
- Fire Safety Clearance from BFP

**Spill Prevention:**
- Secondary containment (dikes, berms) around all storage tanks
- Containment capacity: 110% of largest tank volume
- Daily visual inspection of tanks and containment areas
- Monthly leak detection monitoring (groundwater wells)
- Proper labeling of all tanks and pipes

**Spill Response:**
1. Stop the source of the spill immediately
2. Contain the spill using absorbent materials or berms
3. Notify DENR-EMB within 24 hours for spills reaching water bodies
4. Clean up contaminated soil
5. File incident report

**Vapor Management:**
- Floating roof tanks or vapor recovery systems
- No venting of vapors to atmosphere in populated areas

### 4.3 Coal Handling
**Key Environmental Concerns:**
- Air emissions (dust, SO2 from low-quality coal)
- Water contamination from runoff
- Land use for stockpiles
- Noise

**Required Environmental Compliance:**
- ECC or CNC from DENR-EMB
- Air quality permit if emissions exceed thresholds
- Dust suppression plan
- Storm water management plan for site

**Dust Control:**
- Water sprinkling on active stockpiles and transfer points
- Wind barriers on stockpile perimeter
- Enclosed transfer stations where feasible
- Dust monitoring at site boundary (PM10, PM2.5)

**Water Management:**
- Clean water diversion channels around site
- Sediment basins for runoff from coal areas
- Oil interceptor for equipment areas
- Discharge monitoring (pH, TSS, heavy metals)

**Stockpile Management:**
- Maximum stockpile height limits per approval
- Progressive rehabilitation of inactive areas
- Slope stabilization to prevent erosion

### 4.4 Palm Kernel Shells (PKS)
**Key Environmental Concerns:**
- Particulate matter from handling
- Potential for spontaneous combustion (fire risk)
- Leachate from weathered PKS

**Required Environmental Compliance:**
- DENR-EMB compliance for storage facility
- Fire safety clearance
- Phytosanitary compliance (for export)

**Handling:**
- Enclosed or semi-enclosed handling systems preferred
- Dust extraction systems at transfer points
- Water misting for active loading areas

**Storage:**
- Hardstanding surface (concrete or paved)
- Drainage to contain runoff
- Fire breaks between stockpile sections
- Temperature monitoring for early fire detection

### 4.5 General Warehouse/Depot Operations
**Requirements:**
- Impervious flooring in handling areas
- Proper drainage with oil-water separator
- Waste segregation (general waste, recyclable, hazardous)
- Hazardous waste storage area with secondary containment
- Pest control plan (especially for woodchips/biomass)

## 5. WASTE MANAGEMENT

### 5.1 Waste Classification
| Waste Type | Examples | Disposal |
|-----------|----------|----------|
| General Waste | Paper, plastics, food waste | LGU landfill / recycling |
| Recyclable | Metal scrap, clean wood | Authorized recycler |
| Hazardous | Used oil, oily rags, batteries | TSD facility (DENR-accredited) |
| Special Waste | Ash, debris from cleanup | Per LGU requirement |

### 5.2 Used Oil Management
- Collect in labeled drums
- Store on impervious surface with containment
- Dispose through DENR-accredited TSD facility
- Manifest required for transport

### 5.3 Wastewater
- All wastewater treated before discharge
- Monthly discharge quality monitoring
- Discharge limits per DENR-EMB permit

---

## 6. AIR QUALITY MANAGEMENT

### 6.1 Sources of Emissions
- Diesel fuel combustion (vehicles, generators)
- Dust from ore, coal, PKS handling
- Vapors from diesel storage
- Vehicle exhaust on site roads

### 6.2 Control Measures
- Regular vehicle emission testing
- Speed limits on unpaved roads (25 kph max)
- Watering of haul roads (minimum twice daily)
- Regular maintenance of equipment to minimize emissions

### 6.3 Monitoring
- Visual dust observations recorded daily
- Stack emissions testing annually (if required by permit)
- Ambient air quality monitoring at site boundary

---

## 7. BIODIVERSITY & LAND MANAGEMENT

### 7.1 Protected Areas
- No operations within 1 km of protected areas without special clearance
- No operations in critical habitats
- Annual biodiversity assessment for sites near ecologically sensitive areas

### 7.2 Land Rehabilitation
- Progressive rehabilitation as areas become inactive
- Topsoil salvage and storage for later use
- Revegetation with native species
- Land rehabilitation certificate upon closure

### 7.3 Tree Planting
- Tree replacement ratio per DENR/MGB requirements
- Minimum 1 tree planted per 100 sq meters disturbed
- Survival rate monitoring for 3 years after planting

## 9. ENVIRONMENTAL INCIDENTS

### 9.1 Incident Classification
| Level | Description | Response |
|-------|-------------|----------|
| Minor | Contained on-site; no reach to water | Internal response; report to Safety Officer |
| Moderate | Reached water body; <100L spill | Internal response + DENR-EMB notification within 24h |
| Major | Large spill or ongoing release | Emergency response + DENR-EMB + LGU notification |

### 9.2 Incident Response
1. Stop source of environmental impact
2. Contain the release
3. Notify Environmental Officer / CEO
4. Assess and classify the incident
5. Report to regulatory authorities if required
6. Clean up and remediation
7. Investigate and implement corrective actions
8. Document and close out report
