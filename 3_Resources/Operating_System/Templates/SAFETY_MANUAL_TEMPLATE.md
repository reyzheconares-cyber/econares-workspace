## 1. PURPOSE & SCOPE

### 1.1 Purpose
This Safety Manual establishes health and safety protocols for all personnel involved in the procurement, handling, transport, storage, and delivery of commodities handled by ECONARES. The goal is zero incidents and full regulatory compliance.

### 1.2 Scope
Applies to all ECONARES personnel, contractors, and third-party service providers involved in:
- Nickel/Copper Ore loading and unloading
- Diesel storage and transfer operations
- Coal and PKS bulk handling
- Port and warehouse operations
- Logistics and transport

## 2. SAFETY POLICY STATEMENT

ECONARES is committed to providing a safe and healthy work environment for all employees and contractors. Safety is a core business value and takes priority over production targets and schedules.

Key Principles:
- All workplace injuries are preventable
- No work shall be performed that is believed to be unsafe
- Employees have the right to refuse unsafe work

## 4. GENERAL SAFETY RULES

### 4.1 Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)
All personnel on-site must wear:
- Hard hat (construction/port areas)
- Safety boots (steel-toed)
- High-visibility vest or clothing
- Gloves (task-specific)
- Eye protection (when required)
- Dust mask or respirator (ore/coal/PKS handling areas)

### 4.2 General On-Site Rules
- No smoking within 50 meters of fuel storage or diesel loading areas
- No open flames near diesel or coal storage
- Always maintain clear walkways and pathways
- Report hazards immediately
- Know the location of fire extinguishers and emergency exits
- Do not operate machinery without proper training and authorization
- Stay clear of suspended loads

## 5. COMMODITY-SPECIFIC SAFETY PROCEDURES

### 5.1 Nickel/Copper Ore Handling
**Hazards:** Heavy equipment, dust exposure, uneven terrain, pinch points

Safety Requirements:
- Dust masks (N95 or equivalent) required in handling areas
- Hearing protection near crushers/conveyors
- Safety harness when working at heights above 2 meters
- Buddy system for enclosed space entry
- Regular dust suppression (water spraying)

Emergency Response:
- Dust inhalation: Move to fresh air immediately; seek medical attention
- Injury from heavy equipment: Do not move victim; call emergency services

### 5.2 Diesel Storage and Transfer
**Hazards:** Flammability, vapor inhalation, spills, high-pressure transfer

Safety Requirements:
- No smoking within 100 meters
- Proper grounding and bonding before transfer operations
- Fire extinguisher (CO2 or foam) within 10 meters of transfer point
- Spill containment trays must be in place
- Proper ventilation in enclosed storage areas
- No mobile phone use within 25 meters of transfer operations

Personal Protective Equipment:
- Chemical-resistant gloves
- Safety goggles or face shield
- Flame-resistant clothing

Emergency Response:
- Fire: Use CO2 or foam extinguisher; do NOT use water
- Spill: Contain with absorbent materials; notify DENR-EMB for reportable spills
- Vapor inhalation: Move to fresh air; seek medical attention

### 5.3 Coal and PKS Bulk Handling
**Hazards:** Dust explosions, spontaneous combustion, confined spaces, heavy equipment

Safety Requirements:
- No open flames or hot work within 25 meters of coal/PKS storage
- Regular temperature monitoring of stockpiles (watch for self-heating)
- Dust suppression systems active during loading
- Confined space entry permit required for silos and enclosed holds
- Atmospheric testing (O2, CO, LEL) before enclosed space entry

Spontaneous Combustion Indicators:
- Visible smoke or vapor from stockpile
- Odor of burning
- Temperature above 60 deg C in monitoring wells
Action: Notify Safety Officer immediately; isolate area; do NOT use water on hot coal

### 5.4 Port and Marine Operations
**Hazards:** Falls overboard, mooring accidents, cargo shift, confined spaces on vessels

Safety Requirements:
- Personal flotation device (PFD) worn at all times on docks and vessels
- Proper mooring line handling procedures
- Secure gangway with handrails before boarding vessels
- Three-point contact when climbing ladders
- cargo securing and lashing per cargo plan

## 6. HEAVY EQUIPMENT SAFETY

### 6.1 Authorized Operators
Only personnel with valid licenses and training may operate:
- Forklifts
- Excavators and loaders
- Conveyor systems
- Crane and hoist equipment

### 6.2 Pre-Operation Checklist
Before operating any equipment:
- [ ] Walk-around inspection completed
- [ ] All guards and safety devices in place
- [ ] Lights and signals operational
- [ ] Brakes and steering functional
- [ ] Load chart reviewed
- [ ] Lift plan prepared for heavy lifts

### 6.3 Rigging and Lifting
- All rigging gear inspected before use (slings, shackles, spreader bars)
- Rigging gear with visible damage removed from service
- Safe Working Load (SWL) clearly marked and never exceeded
- Rigging plan for lifts above 1,000 kg or complex lifts
- No suspended loads over personnel

## 7. HEALTH & HYGIENE

### 7.1 Occupational Health
- Baseline and periodic medical examinations for field personnel
- Hearing conservation program for high-noise areas (above 85 dB)
- Respiratory protection program for dust-exposed workers
- Heat stress management in tropical conditions

### 7.2 Hygiene Facilities
- Clean toilet and washing facilities at all work sites
- Potable drinking water available
- Eating areas separate from work/materials areas
- First aid kit on-site at all times

## 8. EMERGENCY RESPONSE PROCEDURES

### 8.1 Emergency Contacts
| Emergency | Contact |
|-----------|---------|
| Fire | Bureau of Fire Protection (BFP) |
| Medical Emergency | Philippine Red Cross / nearest hospital |
| Oil/Hazmat Spill | DENR-EMB 24-hr hotline / Bataan Emergency |
| Police | Philippine National Police |
| Rescue | CDRRMO (Cebu City) |

### 8.2 Incident Reporting
All incidents must be reported within 24 hours:
1. Secure the scene
2. Render first aid / call emergency services
3. Notify Safety Officer and CEO
4. Complete Incident Report Form
5. Investigate root cause within 7 days
6. Implement corrective actions

### 8.3 Evacuation Procedures
- Know your evacuation routes and assembly points
- Account for all personnel at assembly point
- Do not re-enter until cleared by Safety Officer

## 9. CONTRACTOR SAFETY

All contractors working on ECONARES-related operations must:
- Provide proof of safety training for all personnel
- Submit their own safety plan before work commencement
- Use properly maintained equipment
- Report incidents to ECONARES Safety Officer immediately
- Comply with all ECONARES safety rules
