## 1. PURPOSE & SCOPE

### 1.1 Purpose
This Quality Control Manual establishes standards, procedures, and responsibilities governing the procurement, handling, and delivery of commodities traded by ECONARES to ensure products meet contractual specifications and buyer requirements.

### 1.2 Scope
Applies to all commodities handled by ECONARES: Nickel Ore (Limonite), Copper Ore, Diesel (Industrial/Fuel Grade), Coal (Indonesian 5,500-7,000 GAR; Philippine Coal), Palm Kernel Shells (PKS), Woodchips, and Palm Oil.

---

## 2. QUALITY POLICY

ECONARES is committed to delivering commodities that meet or exceed buyer specifications:
- Transparent sampling and testing procedures
- Third-party inspection agency (TPIA) verification for major shipments
- Full traceability from source to delivery
- Continuous improvement through supplier performance monitoring

Approved By: CEO, ECONARES | Review Frequency: Annually

## 5. PRE-SHIPMENT INSPECTION PROCEDURES

### 5.1 General Steps
1. Lot Identification - Confirm lot number, source mine/depot, and quantity
2. Visual Inspection - Check for contamination, foreign materials, moisture evidence
3. Sampling - Obtain representative samples per applicable standard (ISO/ASTM)
4. Sample Splitting - Minimum 3 samples per lot: Seller, Buyer, Referee
5. Sealing and Labeling - Each sample sealed with Lot No., Date, Origin, Analyst initials
6. TPIA Engagement - Appoint SGS, Bureau Veritas, or Inspectorate
7. Draft COQ - Compare against contracted specifications

### 5.2 TPIA Contacts
- SGS Philippines - Manila, Cebu, Davao
- Bureau Veritas - Manila
- Inspectorate - Cebu area

## 6. LABORATORY TESTING

### 6.1 Internal Testing
- Moisture content (drying oven method)
- Visual grading
- Bulk density estimation

### 6.2 External Testing Standards
| Commodity | Tests |
|-----------|-------|
| Nickel/Copper Ore | XRF Assay |
| Coal | Proximate Analysis (ASTM D3172) |
| Diesel | Flash Point, Sulfur, Density (ASTM D975) |
| PKS | NCV, Moisture, Ash, Chlorides |

## 8. NON-CONFORMANCE

Triggers: Out-of-spec assay, excess moisture, contamination, wrong grade.

Procedure: Hold Notice, Root Cause, Corrective Action, NCR Filing, Supplier Notification
