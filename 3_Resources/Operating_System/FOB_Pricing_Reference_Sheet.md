## 7. COPPER CONCENTRATE

**FOB Origin: Indonesia | Philippines**

| Grade | Cu % | FOB Reference (USD/DMT) | Notes |
|-------|------|--------------------------|-------|
| Standard | 25-30% Cu | ~$7,000-9,000/t FOB | TC/RC ~$70-90/t |

**Penalty elements (deduct if present):**
- Arsenic (As) > 0.5% — MAJOR penalty
- Antimony (Sb) > 0.2% — penalty
- Mercury (Hg) > 1 ppm — strict penalty
- Fluorine (F) > 0.1% — penalty

**By-product credits (add to value):** Gold (Au) 1-5 g/t, Silver (Ag) 30-100 g/t
**TC/RC:** Treatment Charge ~USD 70-90/t conc.; Refining Charge ~USD 0.07-0.09/lb payable Cu
**ID export ban:** Copper concentrate exports banned in Indonesia since Jan 2025.
**PH sources:** Tampakan (unmined), Didipio (active under FTAA).
