## SECTION 0: MARKET INTELLIGENCE BRIEF

### Philippines
- World's largest nickel ore producer; major copper and gold mining sector
- Active players: Carmen Copper Corporation (Atlantis Mining), TVI Resource Development, Philchrome Mining, Boreo Resources
- Diesel demand: Petron, independent fuel distributors, industrial/manufacturing plants in Mvisayas and Luzon
- Coal: Captive power plants in mining operations, cement plants, independent power producers
- Decision-maker: VP Procurement / Supply Chain Manager / Operations Director
- Key pain: Logistics delays from mine to port, inconsistent grade, slow pricing response from traditional suppliers
- Advantage for ECONARES: Domestic proximity (Cebu to Visayas mining operations), fast turnaround

### Japan
- Major copper concentrate and coal importer; smelter groups: JX Advanced Metals, Mitsubishi Materials, Sumitomo Metal Mining
- Trading houses (Mitsubishi Corp, Mitsui, Sumitomo, Itochu, Marubeni) intermediate most commodity flows — target these for long-term supply agreements
- Japanese smelters seeking diversified Philippine and SEA sources amid Indonesia DMO policy disruptions
- Decision-maker: Commodity Manager, General Manager — Raw Materials Procurement
- Key pain: Indonesian coal DMO restricting exports; Indonesia nickel ore export bans disrupting supply chains; Japanese buyers actively seeking alternative SEA suppliers
- Advantage for ECONARES: Direct Philippines-SEA sourcing as hedge against Indonesia supply risk

### China
- World's largest copper consumer and nickel processor; Yunnan Copper, Jiangxi Copper, Tongling Nonferrous Metals
- Southern China (Guangdong, Guangxi, Yunnan) buyers prefer SEA-sourced material — shorter logistics, competitive pricing
- Coal: Coastal power plants and cement factories in Guangdong, Fujian
- Decision-maker: Purchasing Manager, Trading Manager — Raw Materials Department
- Key pain: Feedstock security, freight costs, geopolitical tariff risk on Australian and African material
- Advantage for ECONARES: CFR/CIF terms to South China ports competitive vs. longer routes

### South Korea
- Major copper importer; Korea Zinc, LS-Nikko Copper, Samsung C&T Trading
- Growing energy demand from AI data centers and semiconductor fabs (TSMC Korea, Samsung Hwaseong) driving diesel and fuel oil demand
- Coal: POSCO Energy and other independent power producers; steel sector coal needs
- Decision-maker: Senior Buyer — Metals/Energy, General Manager Procurement
- Key pain: Limited SEA supply relationships, dependence on long-established channels
- Advantage for ECONARES: Fast logistics coordination to Busan, Ulsan ports; growing Korean interest in Philippines-originated nickel and copper

### Southeast Asia (Indonesia, Vietnam, Thailand)
- Indonesia: Largest coal exporter globally; but domestic DMO requirements mean captive coal plants (especially nickel smelter captive power) still import; significant diesel distribution market
  - Key buyers: PT Indonesia Power, PT PLN, captive miners (PT ANTAM, PT Trimegah Bangun Persada, PT G RESOURCES GROUP)
  - Nickel ore buyers: Indonesian nickel smelter operators (PT PWM, PT IMIP, PT Virtue Dragon) sourcing domestic and PNG
- Vietnam: Fastest-growing coal demand in SEA; Vietnam Electricity (EVN), Vinacomin; diesel distributors
  - Key pain: Vietnam-Philippines coal trade underexplored; strong opportunity
- Thailand: Supporting industries, cement, fuel distribution; PTT and independent traders
- Decision-maker: Head of Procurement, Trading Desk Manager
- Advantage for ECONARES: Established Philippines-Vietnam and Philippines-Indonesia trade lanes; growing PKS and coal demand in Vietnam
