SECTION 5: MARKET CONTEXT 2025-2026
================================================================================

- China remains the world's largest nickel ore importer, dominated by supply from Indonesia and the Philippines.
- Philippine limonite ore (0.5-1.8% Ni) is primarily used for NPI (Nickel Pig Iron) — the main stainless steel input in China.
- Indonesian ore is higher-grade (1.5-2.0% Ni). Philippine limonite at 40% Fe is ideal for NPI blending.
- Tsingshan Group alone consumes an estimated 80-100 million WMT of nickel ore per year across its operations.
- Chinese buyers procure through long-term supply agreements, quarterly tenders, and spot purchases.
- Philippine ore is price-competitive with shorter shipping distances to Southern/Eastern China vs. Indonesia.
- Tawi-Tawi is a well-established shipping point in the Philippine nickel ore trade — Chinese buyers are familiar with it.
- Current window: Chinese NPI producers are actively building stocks ahead of Q3 2026 production season.
