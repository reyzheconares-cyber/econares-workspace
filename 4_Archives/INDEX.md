# 4_Archives

> **Inactive, completed, and superseded material.** Archived doesn't mean deleted — it means off your active radar.

## Archive Rules

- Projects that are **Closed Won**, **Closed Lost**, or **cancelled** → here
- Weekly reports older than 4 weeks → here
- DSR reports older than 2 weeks → here
- Superseded files (old versions replaced by new) → here

## Current Archive Sections

### DSR_Reports/
Deal Status Reports — archived after deal conclusion or weekly cycle close.

### Weekly_Reports/
Old weekly market intelligence and sales reports.

### Audit_Logs/
System reports — Gmail sync logs, HubSpot sync reports.

### Completed_Deals/
Archived project folders after deal closure.

## How to Archive

1. Move files from source folder to appropriate archive subfolder
2. Add entry below with date archived and outcome
3. Update the source folder's INDEX.md
4. Do NOT delete — archive only
