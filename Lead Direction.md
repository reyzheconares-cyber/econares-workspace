## New lead direction for Econares

Your current prospecting base is still usable, but it needs a cleaner split between accounts that are worth another touch and accounts that should be replaced with fresh targets. Right now, the strongest next-source pools are power and cement companies with procurement, purchasing, supply chain, and fuel-related roles.


### What this means

The fastest way to find better leads is not to widen outreach randomly. It is to tighten your target list around companies already matching power, utilities, oil and energy, building materials, glass, ceramics, and concrete profiles, then focus contact discovery on procurement and purchasing titles first, with supply chain and alternative fuels roles as secondary targets.



### Where to pull the next batch from

Build the next batch from three pools in this order:

1. **New power accounts first** — prioritize utilities, oil and energy, and power-generation related companies.
2. **Cement and building-materials accounts second** — especially companies with kiln fuel, alternative fuels, purchasing, or procurement functions.
3. **Re-check partially worked accounts** — only where the contact title is strong and the company still looks like a fit.

### How to improve lead quality immediately

- Use company type and industry as the first screen before adding any new contacts.
- Prioritize contacts with titles like procurement manager, procurement officer, purchasing officer, supply chain manager, fuel manager, and alternative fuels manager.
- De-prioritize generic inboxes unless they are the only reachable entry point.
- Mark true non-responsive accounts consistently so they do not keep mixing with fresh leads.
- Use your custom outreach stage more consistently so you can tell which accounts are still in research, already worked, or ready to close out as no-response.

### Suggested next move

Take the next 20 to 30 leads from power companies first, then 10 to 15 from cement. For each company, add one senior procurement contact and one operational or fuel-related contact so you are not relying on a single inbox or title.
