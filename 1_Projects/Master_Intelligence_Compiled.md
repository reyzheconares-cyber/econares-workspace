|---|---|
| Above contracted spec range | **BONUS** — buyer pays premium above base price |
| Within contracted spec range | **BASE PRICE** — no adjustment |
| Below contracted spec range | **PENALTY** — price deduction applied |

## 4.1 Pipeline Stages

The ECONARES pipeline follows 7 stages. All deal progress is logged in HubSpot.

### Stage 0 — Prospect Research
- Identify decision-maker (C-level or Head of Procurement ONLY)
- Gather company intelligence: registry filings, annual reports, trade data
- Map buying parameters: volume, grade specs, price benchmark, logistics
- Check existing HubSpot record — DO NOT DUPLICATE
- Create HubSpot contact record: Email, First Name, Last Name, Job Title, Company, Phone, City, Country
- Create HubSpot deal at $0 value with stage: Appointment Scheduled

### Stage 1 — First Outreach
- Choose channel: Call/SMS → Email → WhatsApp (in that order)
- Fraser positioning: Inquiry-first, curiosity-based, 10-15 min call ask
- **Never mention MGB/DENR in first contact**
- Log activity in HubSpot

### Stage 2 — Qualification
- Confirm volume, grade specs, price benchmark, logistics preference, payment terms, buying entity
- Flag MGB/DENR compliance requirement — discuss NOW
- Update HubSpot deal: value and stage Qualified to Buy

### Stage 3 — Proposal and Pricing
- Draft spec sheet and pricing proposal
- Include bonus/penalty clause for coal
- Add MGB/DENR compliance clause
- Send proposal; update HubSpot stage: Proposal Sent

### Stage 4 — Negotiation
- Track counter-offers; hold margin floor; negotiate volume discount
- Log every call, email, counter-offer in HubSpot activity feed
- Update stage: Negotiation in Progress

### Stage 5 — Contract and LOI
- Issue LOI/MOA with: commodity, volume, grade, price, delivery terms, payment term, schedule, inspection clause, compliance clause, force majeure, dispute resolution
- Scan and upload signed LOI to HubSpot
- Update stage: Contract Sent

### Stage 6 — Execution and Logistics
- Confirm supplier readiness, arrange export permit (MGB/DENR), book vessel, coordinate logistics
- Provide SGS loading inspection report to buyer; submit shipping documents for payment release
- Update stage: Closed Won

### Stage 7 — Post-Deal and Retention
- Request referrals; schedule 30-day and 90-day follow-up
- Update stage: Closed Won; add to re-engagement sequence
