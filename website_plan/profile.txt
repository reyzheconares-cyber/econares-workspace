 ECONARES Construction and Trading Services

 THE COMPANY

                ECE Construction and Resources OPC is a Construction & Trading
 company duly registered by the Securities and Exchange Commission under
 Philippine Laws that has strong partnerships with local and international
 companies to provide optimum services and cater best quality products.

                One of its subsidiary is ECE Resources and General Services which
 was registered on 2015 under the Department of Trade and Industry that offers
 various best quality products in bulk such as local and imported industrial fuels in
 different specification like steam coal, metallurgical coke, palm kernel shell and
 woodchips to suffice the huge demand of sugar mills, cement plants,
 manufacturing plants and energy-generating power plants in the country. Aside
 from industrial fuel, it also supplies different kinds of industrial raw materials,
 metallic and non-metallic minerals like construction aggregates (screened &
 crushed), marine sand, boulders/armour rocks, diorite, limestones, chromite,
 nickel, iron sand, silica sand, greywackes, pozzolan, copper, clinker,
 quicklime/hydrated lime, gypsum, anhydrite, and other mine mineral resources.

                In 2019, ECE Construction Services was established under the
 Department of Trade and Industry with Regular Contractor's License from
 Philippine Contractors Accreditation Board (PCAB), as another subsidiary
 company, that handles all types of Construction and Engineering Works in both
 Horizontal and Vertical Fields such as land development and housing works,
 reclamation projects, road networks, ports & bridges, shipyards, industrial &
 manufacturing plant construction, structural steel fabrication & installation,
 commercial buildings & civil works, electrical works, mechanical /
 electromechanical engineering, cooling system/ventilation installation, water
 treatment facilities, deep well & water supply filtration, surveying & designing and
 mineral processing plant installation.
