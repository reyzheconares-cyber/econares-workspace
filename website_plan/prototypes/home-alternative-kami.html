Manifesto

We are a SEC-registered Philippine construction and trading company headquartered in Talisay City, Cebu. We supply steam coal, metallurgical coke, palm kernel shell, woodchips, and seventeen metallic and non-metallic minerals to cement, sugar, and power-generation plants across the country. We build ports, plants, and civil works under our PCAB license. We charter the marine fleet that ships it all.

The objective of this company is to provide high quality construction and engineering services to Philippine industry that could help the national economic progress and development in the country. However, it has always the objective and the will to protect the environment and preserve life while using its resources wisely and prudently for the benefit of all mankind and the many generations to come.

Industrial fuels

The energy inputs that keep cement kilns, sugar mills, biomass power plants, and industrial boilers running across the country.

ECONARES supplies four primary industrial fuels at bulk scale, with quality/quantity inspection at load and discharge on every vessel. Source origins include Indonesia, Australia, South Africa, Malaysia, and Vietnam — selected per customer specification and current market price.

  • Steam coal — 5,500–6,500 kcal/kg NCV, 12–15% moisture, 0–50 mm. For cement kilns and power.
  • Metallurgical coke — CSR 60% min, CRI 25% max. For ferro-alloy and steel.
  • Palm kernel shell — 3,800–4,200 kcal/kg. Biomass substitute for coal.
  • Woodchips — G30 / G50 grades. For biomass power and pulp.
