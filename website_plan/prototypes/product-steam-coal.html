Primary applications

What our customers use it for

  • Cement kilns — primary fuel for clinker production
  • Power generation — thermal power plants, captive and utility
  • Industrial boilers — sugar mills, paper mills, food processing
  • Co-firing with biomass in cement and power applications
Quality assurance

How we test every shipment

  • Pre-shipment analysis at the load port by independent surveyor
  • Quality/quantity inspection at discharge
  • NCV, total moisture, ash, sulfur, volatile matter on every vessel
  • Size analysis by sieve test on every shipment
  • Full certificate of analysis (COA) delivered with shipping documents
